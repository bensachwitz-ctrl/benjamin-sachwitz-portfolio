I
Lloyd's Market Developer Workspace · London · 2025

I underwrite commercial trucking and logging risk across the Southeast. These are accounts you can't judge from a spreadsheet alone, so I put in the time on the safety data and the operations to really understand each one.

During my training at Price Forbes on the Lloyd's of London floor, I saw how complex cargo and property risks are placed in the world's oldest specialty room. Today, I use that experience to underwrite durable risks at Swamp Fox Agency.

I build software that turns fiddly, annoying workflows into something clean and reliable. Native iOS apps in Swift, full-stack React sites, whatever the problem needs. I try to write code that's easy to come back to and quietly does its job.

Coming at it from a business and risk background makes me build a little differently. I care about getting the data right, keeping the screen uncluttered, and not overcomplicating things.
