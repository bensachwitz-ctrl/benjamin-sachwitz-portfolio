Lloyd's Market · London · 2025

A Darla Moore School of Business graduate (Dec 2025) who places the commercial trucking and logging risk that standard markets decline. My time at Price Forbes inside the Lloyd's of London market gave me direct exposure to marine and property placement in the world's most sophisticated risk environment.

At Swamp Fox Agency, I underwrite commercial trucking and logging risk, pricing accounts against loss trends, FMCSA compliance data, and live market dynamics. I bring technical rigor to every file I touch and build systems that compound that rigor across the book.
